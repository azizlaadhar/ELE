Etablissement Laadhar d’Électricité, commonly known as ELE, is a Tunisian electrical contracting and engineering company founded in 1976. Established by Mr. Laadhar, the company built its reputation over decades through high-quality electrical installations, strong technical execution, and reliability on complex public and private infrastructure projects. ELE became one of the pioneering electrical installation companies in Tunisia, especially during the 1990s and early 2000s, a period during which the company contributed to major national facilities including airports, universities, hospitals, hotels, public institutions, pumping stations, and large commercial buildings.

ELE’s original growth was driven by technical excellence rather than marketing. The company was known directly by major construction companies, public institutions, and project owners because of its ability to deliver demanding electrical works with quality, safety, and professionalism. This reputation allowed ELE to become a trusted partner for large-scale projects across Tunisia.

Company Identity

ELE is positioned as a heritage electrical engineering company with international experience and a strong technical foundation. Its identity is built around three major pillars:

Quality: ELE has always prioritized durable, reliable, and high-standard electrical installations. The company’s reputation was built on workmanship and trust.

Heritage: Founded in 1976, ELE carries nearly five decades of experience in the Tunisian electrical contracting market. This gives the company credibility, technical maturity, and a strong portfolio of references.

International ambition: Since 2008, ELE expanded beyond Tunisia and started participating in major international projects, particularly in Africa, including several large-scale projects in Congo Brazzaville and Pointe-Noire.

A strong positioning statement for ELE could be:

“Powering excellence across borders with a legacy of innovation.”

What ELE Does

ELE specializes in the design, installation, integration, and maintenance of electrical and technical systems for large infrastructure and building projects. Its work covers both heavy electrical infrastructure and low-current systems.

The company’s core services include:

Electrical installation in buildings
ELE installs complete electrical systems for public buildings, hotels, office towers, commercial buildings, universities, hospitals, and large private facilities.
Public road electrical installation
ELE can participate in street lighting, public infrastructure electrification, and road-related electrical works.
Fire protection and alarm systems
The company installs fire detection, alarm, and safety systems, including addressable fire alarm systems, detectors, CMSI/CDI systems, and firefighting networks.
Hydraulic pumping station systems
ELE has experience in electrical and hydraulic equipment for pumping stations, including medium-voltage power supply, pump control, valves, anti-water hammer equipment, and centralized management systems.
Data and communication networks
ELE installs structured cabling, IT networks, switches, telephone systems, CCTV, access control, anti-intrusion systems, TV distribution, sound systems, and teleconference systems.
Building management and automation systems
Several ELE projects include centralized technical management systems, known as GTC, used to monitor and manage electrical and technical equipment in complex buildings.
National Experience in Tunisia

ELE has a strong national footprint in Tunisia. The company participated in strategic infrastructure projects that are essential to the country’s public services, tourism, transportation, education, healthcare, and water supply sectors.

Some of ELE’s major Tunisian references include:

Djerba-Zarzis International Airport
ELE contributed to the extension of electrical works at the airport for OACA, including medium-voltage equipment, low-voltage equipment, generators, electrical cabinets, and centralized technical management systems.

Sfax-Thyna International Airport
ELE also participated in the extension of electrical works at Sfax-Thyna International Airport, reinforcing the airport’s electrical infrastructure and safety systems.

Nour Palace Hotel, Mahdia
ELE worked on the construction of the five-star Nour Palace Hotel, a major 1,100-bed hospitality project in Mahdia. The scope included medium-voltage equipment, low-voltage systems, backup generators, fire safety, IT networks, and centralized technical management.

SONEDE water production and pumping station project
ELE participated in the reinforcement of potable water production for the Sahel and Sfax regions, including the Mahdia network extension and the Billy raw water pumping station. This project involved electrical, hydraulic, fire safety, and remote management systems.

SUPCOM Extension Project
ELE worked on the electrical lot for the extension of the Higher School of Communications, SUPCOM, for the Ministry of Communication Technologies and Digital Economy. The project included electrical equipment, UPS systems, computer networks, and sound systems for amphitheaters.

Maternity and Neonatology Center of Monastir
ELE contributed to a public healthcare project involving electrical systems, backup power, patient call systems, and centralized technical management.

Chamber of Advisors Headquarters in Bardo
ELE handled electrical, fire safety, IT, and telephone networks for the headquarters project.

TOPNET & HOTLINE commercial agency project
ELE contributed to the development of a commercial agency headquarters, including electrical systems, generators, cabinets, and fire safety systems.

These projects show that ELE has experience across critical sectors: airports, hotels, water infrastructure, education, healthcare, telecommunications, and government buildings.

This international experience is one of ELE’s strongest differentiators because it proves that the company can operate outside its home market, adapt to different environments, and deliver complex systems in challenging contexts.

ELE’s international references include major projects in Congo, particularly in Brazzaville and Pointe-Noire.

IGH R+8 Office Building, Pointe-Noire, Congo
ELE worked on a high-rise office building project involving electrical equipment, backup power, fire safety, firefighting networks, IT networks, and surveillance systems.

The project included medium-voltage equipment, parallel generators, safety generators, UPS systems, fire safety, Cisco IT and telephone networks, CCTV, access control, anti-intrusion systems, photovoltaic equipment, TV distribution, sound systems, teleconference systems, and centralized technical management.

These references position ELE as a company capable of delivering turnkey technical packages for hotels, high-rise buildings, and complex facilities across Africa.
